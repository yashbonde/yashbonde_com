The benchmark brief

One prompt. Complete system.

Not a toy extraction task. A test of whether the machine can build the machine.

GRETIL preserves many editorial histories. A verse may live in one paragraph, span several, or share a paragraph with dozens of citations. Root text, commentary, headings and apparatus are often visually adjacent but structurally different.

Success means building a reusable system for the whole corpus, not extracting a convincing sample.

The run loop

What the harness must discover

Scoring vectors

Coverage is the floor, not the win
VECTOR_A01

System integrity

Runs end to end, processes the complete dataset, and reproduces the same database.

VECTOR_B02

Text conservation

Retains source text, including content moved into section, speaker and metadata fields.

VECTOR_C03

Boundary truth

Does not merge neighbouring verses or attach commentary to the wrong root text.

VECTOR_D04

Generalization

Turns discoveries into reusable parser shapes and records exceptions instead of hiding them.
